Canadian Wildfires & Unnatural Disasters

The Unnaturalness of Wildfires

While wildfires can be categorized as natural, usually starting that way, the frequency of which they start are not. Climate change is to blame but there is more to the wildfires than just rising temperatures. Historically, in Canada and the US, there had been efforts to suppress naturally occuring wildfires that help manage the land. Indigenous nations used these practices in sacred ceremonial practices but were banned from doing so. In these pursuits, wildfires today have gotten more intense because of the poor management of land and disregard for the purposeful uses of smaller fires.

As a result, these larger wildfires are "disasters being produced by social conditions". The impact of wildfires to peoples health, livelihood, the environment and communities has caused harm all around as well as being very expensive.
