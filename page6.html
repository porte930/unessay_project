Western Conception of Nature

Western Conception of nature sees humans as abstract from nature, usually to dominate it. Taken from romanticism of the frontier and biblical stories, these ideas that nature must be tamed have had impacts onto the environment. For the North American Wildfires, the need to tame nature, forbidding Indigenous practices of seasonal burnings as well as isolating the forests for parks, recreation and national territory ultimately led to these seasonal mass wildfires.

In their effort to perserve national forests, homes, natural resources and exclude Indigenous communities from their sacred and regenerative practices, the resulting enforcements have had the opposite effect. Now the forests and livelihoods that were to be preserved came back negatively in full force, damaging more of the romanticized nature.

In efforts to combat and restore ecosystems and habitats, the Government of Canada has adopted Indigenous-led practices of prescribed fires (Government of Canada 2025). Climate change and rising temperatures probably will not change, but they do hope to be able to reduce ecological damge caused by ongoing, large forest fires.
