Inspiration Behind Unessay

Silent Hill is a 1999 horror-survival game where we follow Henry Mason, the main protagonist through the town of Silent Hill, Maine, who is in search of his daughter Cheryl. The town is filled with dangerous creatures and puzzles and mysteriously empty.The defining feature of the game is the fog the covers the town. At first, the reason for this design choice is the game rendering. Around this time in video game creation, open-world rednering was limited, especially with the low budget Team Silent - creators of Silent Hill- had. The fog though has become an iconic symbol for the game, gaining meaning over time. Some say this represents the passage between the dream world and reality. Others think it connects to the other side where the dangerous creatures come from. For this assignment and refrences from the game, the fog also represents environmental decay and destruction.

I was inspired to use silent hill to talk about the present environmental concerns of forest fires. Using the game and it references, I explore the ways in which our now annual forest fires will have devestating effects for our future.
