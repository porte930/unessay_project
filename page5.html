Conclusion

Using the theme of Silent Hill and its game inspiration, Wildfires are a very prevalent and modern day effect of climate change and human made errors that cause "natural" disasters. The early romanticizing of the North American frontier, the genocide and removal of Indigenous communities and disregarding nature as intertwined with humanity and not seperate from it has damaged our air, our ecosystems, our homes and livlihood. When every summer, the air is unbreathable and we risk our health going outside, maybe once it can seem like a natural disaster, but our meddling with the land and poor policy plus infrastructure caused these issues.

Silent Hill is just a game about many horrors of the mind and body, bending fiction with reality. Our present day climate issues isn't so different. Sometimes I am mesmerized by how the fog and environmental decay I see every year mimics the landscape of one of my all time favorite games. How a design issue resonates with history and the climate astounded me and I hope to bend reality and fiction, with all its horror. To me our climate disasters are like being in a horror game. One just like Silent Hill.
